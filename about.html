About Vidnex

Built to connect industrial challenges with research talent.

Vidnex is a professional marketplace where industries post real manufacturing or innovation challenges and researchers compete to solve them.

The platform simplifies problem discovery, proposal evaluation, payment handling, and ongoing collaboration between industry teams and research professionals.

From MSMEs to research institutions, Vidnex bridges the gap between industrial need and academic expertise — with structured workflows, trusted payments, and transparent bidding.

Why it works

Clear workflows, transparent bidding, trusted payments, and focused profiles for both researchers and industry teams.
