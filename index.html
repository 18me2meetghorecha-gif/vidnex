How It Works

A simple, transparent process for everyone

1. Post a Problem

Industries describe their technical challenge with scope, budget, timeline, and required expertise. Researchers see detailed problem statements.

2. Receive Bids

Qualified researchers review the problem and submit structured proposals with their approach, experience, timeline, and cost estimates.

3. Select & Collaborate

Industries compare bids, review researcher profiles, and select the best fit. Clear communication channels open for ongoing updates.

4. Secure Payment & Delivery

Payment is processed through Vidnex. Researchers deliver results securely. Funds are released upon industry confirmation of completion.
