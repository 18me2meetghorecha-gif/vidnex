About Us

Built to connect industrial challenges with research talent.

Vidnex is a professional and approachable marketplace where industries can post real manufacturing or innovation challenges and researchers can compete to solve them.

The platform is designed to simplify problem discovery, proposal evaluation, payment handling, and ongoing collaboration between both sides.
